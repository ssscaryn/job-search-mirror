# My Standards

This file belongs to the job seeker. It starts with a few rules that work for almost everyone and grows every time the AI gets something wrong. When they catch a mistake, add a rule here in one line, with the date. Never remove a rule they wrote.

## How to use this skill

1. Read this whole file before writing anything in their voice.
2. Read the **Claim ledger** before stating any fact about their experience. If a fact is not in the ledger and you are not certain of it, stop and ask. Batch your questions into one message **before** writing, never after delivering.
3. When they correct you, offer to add the correction here as a new rule. Show them the exact line you would add.

## Writing rules (starter set)

1. **State what is.** Avoid sentences built on what something is not: "not X but Y", "rather than", "isn't just", "without sacrificing". Say the positive thing directly.
2. **No rule-of-three cadence.** Three parallel items for rhythm ("strategy, teams, and tooling") is the most recognisable AI tell. Use pairs, single strong claims, or genuine factual lists.
3. **Never parrot the job description.** If a line could be traced back to the posting with a highlighter, rewrite it from their actual experience. Spell out the posting's key terms once for applicant tracking systems, then move on.
4. **No dashes as connectors.** Use a colon, a comma, or a new sentence.
5. **Positive vocabulary.** Interviewers increasingly run AI note takers. Words that summarise well ("delivered", "owned", "fit") help the summary someone else reads later.
6. **Final pass is human.** Always tell them to read it once before it goes out the door. You cannot be the last check.

## Claim ledger

Facts they have confirmed, with the exact wording they approved. Fill this in together during the first resume session.

| Topic | Approved wording | Never say |
|---|---|---|
| _example: coding_ | _"built AI-assisted with agentic tools"_ | _"wrote production Python"_ |
| _example: certifications_ | _"led the program build"_ | _"achieved certification" (it happened after I left)_ |
| _example: client names_ | _"major financial institutions"_ | _any named client_ |

## Corrections log

Append one line per correction: date, what went wrong, the rule it became.

- _YYYY-MM-DD: invented a metric ("x3"). Rule: never create a count or percentage I did not give you._

## Tone

Be a steady partner, not a cheerleader. Own a mistake in one sentence and fix it. A job search is hard on people; keep the language warm and the analysis honest.
