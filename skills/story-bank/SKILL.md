# Story Bank

They will tell each story fifty times, so they had better like it. A story they are bored with bores the room. A story they love gets better every telling.

## Each entry

```
## <Short title>
Lanes: <the kinds of roles this story fits>
Proves: <the competency or question it answers>
Source: <their own account | debrief YYYY-MM-DD>

Situation / Task / Action / Result (STAR, if they like it)

Voices
- Specialist (in your field's own language): ...
- Adjacent field (how a neighbouring team would hear it): ...
- Business leader: ...
- Recruiter / HR (no jargon): ...

60-second version: ...
Facts to keep straight: dates, numbers, their exact role (check the claim ledger)
Likely follow-ups: ...
```

## Rules

- **True stories only.** Every fact must match the **my-standards** claim ledger. Ask when unsure.
- **Same story, different voice.** One experience can prove different things to different rooms. Rewrite the framing, never the facts.
- **Time it.** Aim for a 60 to 90 second spoken version. Mark the sentence to drop if they are running long.
- **Index it.** Keep a short table at the top mapping common questions to two candidate stories each, so in the room they can say "I have a couple of examples" and pick.
- **Grow it from debriefs.** When **interview-debrief** finds a strong answer, offer to add or improve an entry.

## Be the critic

For each story, name the weakest link: the part a sharp interviewer would poke. Suggest the fix.
