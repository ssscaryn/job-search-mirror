# Job Triage

A search runs on volume: often a few hundred applications and dozens of interviews over two to three months. Time per application is the budget. This skill decides where it goes.

## Process

1. **Read every posting.** Screenshots are fine. Pull out: company, title, location and onsite days, comp band if listed, posting age or "reposted", and anything they told you they care about (commute, dogs, team size).
2. **Score fit honestly.** Use their tracker if available: which role families actually convert for them? Rank by evidence of what lands, not by which experience they have the most of. If the data and your instinct disagree, say so and trust the data.
3. **Sort into three groups:**
   - **Apply now:** strong fit, quick application (one page, or a short easy apply).
   - **Save for when you are warmed up:** their top choice companies. Early resumes, screeners, and skills interviews produce lessons; spend those on roles that matter less. Watch posting age so these do not expire while they practice.
   - **Park:** anything that needs an account or a multi-page portal. Log it to the tracker's Revisit list **with the reason**. Suggest checking the company's own careers page for another route if fit is strong.
4. **Flag stale postings.** "Reposted" or older than a few weeks: note it, do not hide it.
5. **Hand back a numbered list** with one line of reasoning per job and a time estimate for the session.

## Check the employer and the recruiter

- **Layoffs:** ask whether the company has filed recent WARN layoff notices (public state filings; the Make the Mirror True page checks California's). A match is a lead to verify, never proof.
- **External recruiters:** ask who the client is before agreeing to anything. Log "Client disclosed: No" if they will not say. Never let a recruiter submit the resume without explicit approval; two agencies submitting the same person can disqualify them.
- **Same role, many agencies:** if the same job keeps arriving through different recruiters, note it.
- **Scam patterns:** contact only through Telegram, WhatsApp, or Signal; a personal email domain for a company recruiter; an offer before any interview; requests for an ID number, bank details, or payment; being asked to buy equipment and get reimbursed; pay far above the market band. If two or more appear, tell them plainly to stop and verify the recruiter through the company's own site.

## Reaching

Include a couple of stretch roles each session and label them as reaches. Track how reaches convert so they learn from them either way.

## Be the critic

For each top-ranked job, name the strongest reason they might be screened out. Then rank.
