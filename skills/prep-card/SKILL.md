# Prep Card

The questions they ask are an instrument. Good questions make an interviewer see a peer instead of a candidate, and they pull the interviewer's operating manual into the open.

## Inputs

- Company, role, round, interviewer names and titles
- The exact resume submitted for this job (attach it to the card)
- Notes or debriefs from earlier rounds
- Their story bank and question bank, if they exist

## The card

1. **The room.** Who they are meeting and what each person likely cares about. Look up each interviewer's background (their path, past companies, public writing) and find one authentic point of connection. This is mirroring: reflect their priorities back, generously and honestly.
2. **Their language.** Vocabulary, frameworks, and priorities surfaced in earlier rounds. Use them back in this one.
3. **Likely questions → stories.** Predicted questions (from the posting and earlier rounds) mapped to two stories each from the story bank.
4. **Truth audit of the submitted resume.** Green / yellow / red per claim, with the honest wording ready. If a claim is challenged, they read the bullet aloud before answering.
5. **Questions to ask, ranked.**
   - Softballs that suit anyone at the company (culture, leadership tone, favourite part of working there).
   - Seat-specific questions only this interviewer can answer (the team they would inherit, what success looks like in a year, how risk is weighed).
   - Order them so the questions that earned the best reactions in past interviews come last.
6. **The closer.** Always the final question: *"Is there anything in my resume, my experience, or what we've discussed today that gives you any hesitation, that I could speak to?"* It asks them to show their cards while there is still time to answer.
7. **Watch.** One or two things to be careful of in this room (a known gap, a past stumble, a tough interviewer style).

## Question bank

Keep a running bank of questions they ask, each with how it landed and in which kind of round. Update it from every **interview-debrief**.

## Delivery

If they have a card layout they like, open a past card and match it exactly. Keep live company and interviewer names off anything meant to be shared.
