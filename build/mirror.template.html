Women in Security · November 12

Make the mirror true.

Your job search, told by your own data. Load a tracker to see your funnel, where you stall, your pace, and which postings show warning signs. Everything runs in this page; nothing you load leaves your device.

Where your applications go

Every application, from sent to the furthest round it reached.

What converts

How often each kind of role turned into an interview. Trust the data over your instinct about where your strength is.

Edit lanes

One lane per line: a name, a colon, then words from job titles that belong to it. The first line that matches wins, so put specific lanes first.

Keep the reps up

Applications per week. When interviews ramp up, this is the line that quietly collapses. The dashed line is a floor of 12.

Posting watch

What you observed about each company, from the posting to the offer. These are facts from your own tracker, not verdicts: a long-open role can be a hard seat to fill, or a posting nobody intends to fill. Use them to decide where your limited applications go.

Playbooks for any AI

Eight playbooks, one per part of the talk. They work in ChatGPT, Gemini, or Claude. Each one makes the AI name what fell flat before it is allowed to tell you what went well.

ChatGPTPaste it at the start of a chat, or into a Project's instructions so every chat in it follows the playbook.
GeminiCreate a Gem and paste the playbook as its instructions.
ClaudePaste it into a Project's instructions, or install the skill version from the kit.
