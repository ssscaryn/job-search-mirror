Make the mirror true.

Your job search, told by your own data. Load a tracker to see your funnel, where you stall, your pace, and which postings show warning signs. Everything runs in this page; nothing you load leaves your device.

Your data stays in this browser.

Where your applications go

Every application, from sent to the furthest round it reached.

What converts

How often each kind of role turned into an interview. Trust the data over your instinct about where your strength is.

Edit lanes

One lane per line: a name, a colon, then words from job titles that belong to it. The first line that matches wins, so put specific lanes first.

Keep the reps up

Applications per week. When interviews ramp up, this is the line that quietly collapses. The dashed line is a floor of 12.

Posting watch

What you observed about each company, from the posting to the offer. These are facts from your own tracker, not verdicts: a long-open role can be a hard seat to fill, or a posting nobody intends to fill. Use them to decide where your limited applications go.

Debrief an interview

Paste the transcript from your note taker, or your own notes. You get question-by-question game tape, what fell flat, their operating manual, and a stronger answer for every question. The questions and stories go into your banks, and red flags go to posting watch.

Text, captions (.vtt, .srt), or audio (.m4a, .mp3, .wav). Or paste below.

From your debriefs, most asked first. These predict your next round.

    Questions you ask

    With what each one revealed. Save your best for last, and always close with: "Is there anything that gives you hesitation I could speak to?"

      Story bank

      True stories you'll tell fifty times, so you'd better like them.

      My standards

      Claims you can defend and the wording you've approved, plus writing rules. Every debrief and prep card reads this, so the AI never invents experience. Saved as you type.

      Prep card

      Pick an upcoming interview and get a one-page card: the room, their language, likely questions mapped to your stories, a truth check of the resume you sent, your questions ranked with the strongest last, and the closer. Coming in the next build.

      Playbooks for any AI

      Eight playbooks, one per part of the talk. They work in ChatGPT, Gemini, or Claude. Each one makes the AI name what fell flat before it is allowed to tell you what went well.
